# The importance of Data Organization
When combining data from multiple tables, your data can be all over the place and make a little sense. You may need a lot of time to make sense out of it. Therefore is it really important to organize the data. By simply organizing the data better, you will be able to generate clear reports, better understand relationships and distributions.

**JOINS** are a powerful tool to achieving this, they will allow you to enhance your ability to analyze and report on data effectively.
